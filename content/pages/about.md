Title: About

I’m Ingrid Ivarsen, a historian of Anglo-Saxon law and legal language, with a side-interest in the nineteenth-century study of medieval history. 


This blog is for some of the thoughts and ideas I’ve had over the last eight years that haven't been published. It’s not necessarily aimed at an expert audience, but it’s not an introduction to medieval history either.
